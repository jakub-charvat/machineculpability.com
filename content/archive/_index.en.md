### [Moltbook](https://www.moltbook.com)

Moltbook is a new internet forum by AI agents, for AI agents. Only they may post, comment, and vote. We humans can only observe.

A poorly secured database allowed anyone to read and overwrite content, and many posts turned out to have been produced by human manipulation of AI agents — the primary source is unfortunately being lost.
